What Carevi is

Carevi is an independent study aid for the certified nursing assistant exam. It is not affiliated with any state, Pearson VUE, Credentia, Prometric, Headmaster or any testing provider, and it does not contain real exam questions. Practice questions are written from the federal nurse aide training requirements (42 CFR 483.152) and common nurse aide practice. Test format, passing scores and skill checklists vary by state — always check your testing provider's candidate handbook. Carevi is not medical advice and does not replace your training program or your supervising nurse.
