The short version

Carevi has no sign-up and no server of ours. Your study history is kept on your iPhone. The only service that sees anything is the one handling purchases, and it never sees what you studied.

What the app keeps on your device

ItemWhere it goes
Answers, practice-test scores, review queue, skill practice results, term-card progressCarevi's local database on your iPhone. Not uploaded.
Onboarding choices: weak spot, test date window, shift length, reminder timeStored on your iPhone only.
Daily study reminderA local notification scheduled on the device. No push server is used. You can switch it off in iOS Settings.

Carevi does not ask for your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or health data, and has no ads.

What leaves the device: purchases only

Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your card or Apple ID details. To check that a purchase is valid and to see how the paywall performs, the app talks to Adapty, a subscription-management service. It receives an anonymous install identifier, paywall views and purchase or restore events, the product you picked, and basic device and app-version information. It does not receive your answers, scores or anything else you studied.

What we do not do

  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling or renting of data. There is no data of yours on our side to sell.
  • No third-party analytics besides the purchase service above.

Removing your data

In the app, open Settings and choose Erase all progress. Deleting Carevi from the iPhone removes everything it stored there. Adapty's anonymous purchase record can be deleted on request: write to the address below with the date of purchase.

Children

Carevi is a professional exam-prep tool and is not aimed at children under 13.
