Privacy Policy

Carevi: CNA Exam Prep · Last updated September 26, 2026

Carevi is an offline study app for the certified nursing assistant (CNA) exam. There are no accounts and no server that stores your answers. We don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your answers, practice-test scores, review boxes, skill practice results and term-card progress.
  • What you told us in onboarding: which part of the exam worries you, your test window, your study-break length and reminder time.

This is stored only in Carevi's own storage on your iPhone and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Reminders

The daily study reminder and the note a day before a free trial ends are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Carevi receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases and measure how the paywall performs. It does not receive your answers or study data.

Analytics and tracking

Carevi does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all progress in the app, or delete Carevi from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Children

Carevi is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
